## The small print

Battery figures are continuous local video playback at 50% brightness; streaming
is roughly 24 hours. Trade-in values depend on model and condition, and are
applied at checkout. Satellite messaging requires line of sight to the sky and
is free for five years from activation.

Lumen is a fictional brand. This site exists to exercise Zellij, and everything
on it is invented.
